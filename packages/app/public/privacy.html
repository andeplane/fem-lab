Models and conversations are kept in your browser. When you use hosted AI, the content needed for that request leaves your browser and is processed by FEM Lab and OpenAI. You can use the modeling and solver tools without signing in or using hosted AI.

Local projects and browser storage

FEM Lab stores projects, Journals, app preferences and conversations in browser storage. Opening, editing, meshing or solving a local model does not upload the model to an account archive. Browser storage can be lost if you clear site data or change devices; download important models separately. Signing out or deleting an account does not remove these local files or conversations.

Optional sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and browser session storage for the signed-in session and email-link completion. The application does not use advertising trackers or sell your project content.

Hosted AI requests

Before your first hosted AI request, the Assistant asks you to review its data disclosure. Requests can include your messages, conversation history, system and project instructions, selected model information, relevant project and geometry data, tool definitions and results, and attached images or screenshots. These pass through our service to OpenAI so it can generate a reply or propose Commands. Approved tools execute in your browser.

The FEM Lab account database does not store prompt text, responses, model files, screenshots or conversation transcripts. It stores request identifiers, a request digest for duplicate detection, model names, timestamps, request status, counted usage, reservations and credit receipts. We do not intentionally log request bodies, authentication tokens or images.

Our OpenAI API requests use store: false. That setting is not a zero-retention guarantee: OpenAI can retain content for abuse monitoring, normally for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its policy. API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default. Read the OpenAI API data controls before sending confidential or personal information. The service does not promise EU-only AI processing.

Account, usage and payment records

Firebase Authentication processes your email address, login identity and authentication security information. Our account service records your account identifier, verified email, status, credit grants, usage and billing references to provide access and enforce limits. We receive identity information from the sign-in provider you choose.

Stripe handles hosted checkout, payment methods, invoices and subscription management. Stripe receives the information you provide during checkout, including billing and tax details. Our service stores Stripe customer, subscription and invoice references and payment status; it does not receive or store your full card details. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

We use account and request information to provide the service and carry out the subscription agreement; security and usage records to protect the service, prevent fraud and resolve disputes; and required billing records to meet legal obligations. Providing account information is optional for local modeling, but necessary for hosted AI and subscriptions. We do not use this information for advertising profiles.

Service providers and processing locations

  • Google / Firebase: authentication, application hosting, backend compute, account database, operational logging and temporary account exports. Our Cloud Run service, Cloud SQL database and export bucket are configured in Frankfurt, Germany. Authentication and other Google services have their own processing locations and safeguards; this is not an EU-only processing promise. See Firebase privacy and security information.
  • OpenAI: hosted AI inference and its security and abuse monitoring, under the API data controls linked above.
  • Stripe: payment processing, billing, subscription management and fraud prevention under its privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: domain registration and DNS. The FEM Lab app and AI API are served by Google infrastructure.

These providers can process information outside Norway and the EEA. Their applicable service terms describe international-transfer safeguards. Contact us if you need information about the safeguards that apply to your data.

Retention and account deletion

Local projects and conversations remain in browser storage until you delete them or clear site data. Account identity and email are kept while your account is active. In Account, you can export account records or request deletion after signing in again.

Deletion immediately blocks new AI requests. Background processing stops subscription renewal, reconciles accepted requests, removes temporary exports and deletes your Firebase login. It then clears the email and replaces the login identifier in our account database. This can take longer when a request or payment action is still being resolved.

Pseudonymous usage, credit and billing receipts remain after deletion for accounting, reconciliation and fraud or dispute review. These records do not contain your prompts or local projects. They currently have no automatic expiry in the account ledger. Stripe retains its own payment records under its policy and legal obligations. Operational infrastructure logs and backups follow the relevant Google service retention settings; deleting a live account does not immediately erase every backup copy.

Account export files are private temporary downloads. They expire after 24 hours and are removed by background cleanup. Individual download links expire after at most 15 minutes. Anyone with an unexpired link can use it, so keep the link private.

If processing depends on consent, you can withdraw it; that does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing. We may need to verify your identity and retain records where a legal obligation or another applicable exception requires it.

You can complain to Datatilsynet or your local data protection authority. We will update this notice when our processing changes and show the updated date here.
