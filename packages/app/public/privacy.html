Local and private account projects

Without sign-in, FEM Lab keeps projects and Journals in this browser. New projects created while signed in save to an account-specific browser cache and queue synchronization to private cloud storage. Signing in does not automatically upload existing anonymous projects; “Upload a copy” copies only the project you choose and preserves its anonymous original.

A synchronized project includes its name, identifiers and timestamps, the portable engine Model and Journal, retained solved states and Results, exact input bytes captured by successful imports, and completed generated output files, including their filenames. Selecting a folder does not upload unrelated folder contents. Conversations and app preferences remain separate browser state. Cloud project storage does not itself send your project to the AI provider.

Account projects are private to the authenticated account. A browser cache supports offline editing, and pending changes synchronize when access, connectivity and quota allow. Conflicts keep the browser copy and remote revision for an explicit choice. Signing out stops that account's sync and hides its project rows; cached bytes can remain on the device. Browser storage can be lost if you clear site data or change devices. Download important project bundles separately. Signing out or deleting an account does not erase every browser cache, anonymous project or downloaded file.

Optional sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and browser session storage for the signed-in session and email-link completion. The application does not use advertising trackers or sell your project content.

Hosted AI requests

Before your first hosted AI request, the Assistant asks you to review its data disclosure. Requests can include your messages, conversation history, system and project instructions, selected model information, relevant project and geometry data, tool definitions and results, and attached images or screenshots. These pass through our service to OpenAI so it can generate a reply or propose Commands. Approved tools execute in your browser.

The hosted AI usage ledger does not store prompt text, responses, model files, screenshots or conversation transcripts. It stores request identifiers, a request digest for duplicate detection, model names, timestamps, request status, counted usage, reservations and credit receipts. Private cloud project content is stored separately, as described above; project metadata and storage operation records support ownership, synchronization, quotas and deletion. We do not intentionally log request bodies, authentication tokens or images.

Our OpenAI API requests use store: false. That setting is not a zero-retention guarantee: OpenAI can retain content for abuse monitoring, normally for up to 30 days, with exceptions described in its policy. API data is not used to train OpenAI models by default. Read the OpenAI API data controls before sending confidential or personal information. The service does not promise EU-only AI processing.

Retention and account deletion

Anonymous projects, account-specific caches and conversations can remain in browser storage until you delete them or clear site data. Cloud projects remain until you delete them or request account deletion; there is no scheduled project expiry. Replacing or deleting a cloud revision queues cleanup of old objects. Cleanup retries on failure and has no guaranteed completion deadline. In Account, you can export account records or request deletion after signing in again.

Account deletion immediately freezes cloud project access/sync and new AI requests. Background processing stops subscription renewal, reconciles accepted requests, purges cloud project objects and metadata, removes temporary exports and deletes your Firebase login. Identity removal cannot finish until project purge succeeds; failures leave deletion pending. It then clears the email and replaces the login identifier in our account database. Requests, payments or cleanup still being resolved can delay completion.

Pseudonymous usage, credit and billing receipts remain after deletion for accounting, reconciliation and fraud or dispute review. These usage and billing receipts do not contain your prompts or project file content. They currently have no automatic expiry in the account ledger. Stripe retains its own payment records under its policy and legal obligations. Operational infrastructure logs and backups follow the relevant Google service retention settings; deleting a live account does not immediately erase every backup copy.

Pseudonymous eligibility identifiers, review references, reviewed-email fingerprints and monthly claim markers also remain after account deletion to prevent repeated free allowances. They currently have no automatic expiry. These are pseudonymous records, not a promise of anonymous data. Your account export includes its eligibility and claim records; rebinding eligibility to a new account does not expose a previous account’s ledger. You can contact us about these records and the rights described below.

Your FEM Lab account and any account used to manage a Managed Payments order are separate. For questions or requests about the merchant’s order records, use the payment-support link on your receipt. Deleting your FEM Lab account does not by itself delete that separate account or all records the merchant must retain.

Account-record JSON export includes a cloud project metadata index, not full project artifact bytes, captured files or Results. Download complete projects separately before deletion. Account export files are private temporary downloads. They expire after 24 hours and are removed by background cleanup. Individual download links expire after at most 15 minutes. Anyone with an unexpired link can use it, so keep the link private.
