# FEM Lab documentation

FEM Lab builds finite-element Models, meshes them and solves them in your browser. Use it to explore structural and thermal behavior, compare an idealized model with a known answer, and keep a replayable record of how the analysis was made. Local modeling and solving work without an account. The optional hosted Assistant requires sign-in and an available AI allowance.
